About the property

Description property

Welcome to No. 160 The Park - a wonderful 4 bed semi-detached home located in this popular residential development, walking distance of the bustling town of Naas to include; schools, shops, supermarkets, stylish boutiques, bars, restaurants, health, leisure and public transport facilities.

Accommodation briefly comprises of; entrance hall, guest w.c., living room, kitchen/dining room,, 4 bedrooms, en-suite and main family bathroom.

Outside are spacious front and rear gardens with ample off street parking in the driveway. The rear south east garden is fully walled with timber shed.

The Arrow train station in Sallins and the N7 motorway are also just a few moments away, making commuting to and from Dublin hassle free.

Don't miss the opportunity to view this wonderful family home in Naas.
